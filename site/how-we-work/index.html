How we work

  1. 1. Reach

    We run ads on Facebook and Instagram that reach the owners and decision-makers of B2B companies who could buy what you sell.

  2. 2. Qualification

    Before anyone gets to your sales team, we check that they have the budget, the authority to buy, a real need for your product, and a reason to buy now. People who don't meet all four don't get a call.

  3. 3. Preparation

    Before the call, each prospect learns what you do and why it fits their business. They arrive ready to talk about buying.

  4. 4. Conversion

    We work with your sales team to turn more of these calls into deals, so each lead brings in more revenue.

After the first four months

The first four months are for building the system and proving it works. After that, we stay on as a long-term partner and put more volume through it to keep growing your revenue.
